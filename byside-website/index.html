How it works

Four steps, and the first one is a conversation — not a contract.

Most families are live within ten days. Nothing starts until your parents have met the person who will be visiting them.

01

We talk to you

Free · 45 minutes · video or phone

You tell us who your parents are, what worries you at 2am, and what's already in place. We tell you honestly whether we're the right fit — and what it will cost. No obligation either way.

02

We visit them at home

Within 5 days · in person

A care manager sits with your parents in their own house. We look at health, mobility, the home itself, the medicines, the paperwork — and we listen to what they want, which is not always what you assume.

03

You approve the plan and meet the companion

Day 7 – 10

A written plan: visit days, what's covered, what's extra, who to call. You meet the matched companion on video before the first visit. If your parents don't take to them, we match someone else — no questions, no charge.

04

Care starts, and you stop guessing

Ongoing · weekly log, monthly call

Visits begin. A care log after every visit, a monthly review call scheduled in your time zone, and a real person who picks up when you need them. Cancel any month with 30 days' notice.

Everything on the record

Peace of mind isn't a feeling. It's knowing what happened on Tuesday.

The worst part of caring from far away is the silence between phone calls. We fill it with specifics.

After every visit

The care log

What was done, what was noticed, what's coming up — plus vitals, photos and receipts. Written by the companion who was in the room, not generated. Shared with every family member you add, so no one is the designated messenger.

Every month

The review call

Thirty minutes with your care manager at a time that works in Dubai, Toronto or Bengaluru. Trends rather than incidents: is mobility slipping, is the sleep worse, is it time to talk about a walker, a nurse, or moving a bedroom downstairs.

The moment it matters

The one number

A 24/7 line answered by a person in Kerala who can see your parents' file. Escalation is written into the plan in advance — which hospital, which doctor, which relative, and what your parents themselves have said they want.

Questions families ask

The honest answers.

My parents are proud people. They'll say they don't need this.

Most do, at first. So we don't arrive as carers — we arrive as help with the boring things: the bill, the queue, the hospital paperwork. Companionship follows once there's a relationship, and it's usually your parents who start asking for the extra visit.

We also never present ourselves as your idea alone. Your parents consent to the plan and choose what stays private from you.

Is this a nursing service?

No. BySide companions are trained in elder first aid and dementia awareness, but they are not nurses and they do not give injections, dress wounds or administer treatment. On the Concierge plan a qualified nurse carries out a quarterly assessment.

When clinical care is needed, we find, vet and supervise a home-nursing provider for you — and we stay involved so you're not managing an agency from another country.

What actually happens in an emergency?

You call one number, or we call you. In a live district someone is at the house or hospital typically within the hour. We arrange the ambulance, handle admission, contact the doctor already on file, and call you before we call anyone else.

The escalation plan — which hospital, which doctor, which relative nearby, and what your parents have said they want — is written down when care starts, not improvised at 2am.

How do you handle money?

Companions never touch cash. You top up a care wallet; bills, groceries and vendors are paid digitally from it, and every receipt appears in your log. Your monthly fee is separate and fixed — we take no commission from hospitals, pharmacies, labs or vendors, which is also why we can recommend them honestly.

Can I pay from abroad?

Yes. We accept cards issued in the UAE, UK, US, Canada, Australia and Singapore, and payments from NRE/NRO accounts. Billing is always in Indian rupees so the amount never shifts with an exchange-rate markup, and invoices are GST-compliant.

What if we don't like the companion?

Tell your care manager and we match someone else, at no charge and without asking you to justify it. Fit matters more than credentials in this work — language, temperament, and whether your mother enjoys the hour. You also meet the companion on video before the first visit.

Are you locking me into a contract?

No. Plans are monthly and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. There is no joining fee, no exit fee, and the first conversation and home assessment cost nothing even if you decide not to go ahead.
